# Style guide

Observed on Xiaohongshu tech and tools posts (open-source Mac apps, terminal tips),
comparing high-engagement posts with low-engagement template posts.

## What well-received posts do

- Images are real: app screenshots, screen recordings, ray.so-style code images,
  even phone photos of the monitor. Several top posts had no designed cover at all.
- The cover, when present, is a large plain title plus one subject (the app icon or
  a screenshot). Little text.
- The body is first person and specific: why the author built or uses the thing,
  what bothered them before, the one feature they use most, a real number they
  measured. Ending with a plain question ("你还希望它支持什么？") is common.
- Lists are fine when each item carries the author's own judgement, for example
  "/compact 上下文不够用了就压缩一下", not a feature slogan.
- Roundups that work give each item its own page with a big icon and one line, then
  a "怎么选" summary page.

## What reads as machine-written

- Stock openers and closers: 破防了, 家人们, 宝子们, 绝绝子, yyds, 本程序员忍不了了,
  建议收藏, 收藏不迷路, Star 不吃亏, 最佳选择, 赶紧试试.
- Hype words: 保姆级, 神器, 神仙, 天花板, 丝滑, 炸裂, 封神, 效率翻倍, 一键搞定, 告别 XX.
- Emoji as structure: ✨亮点, ⚡️一行搞定, 1️⃣ 2️⃣ 3️⃣, a column of ✅ selling points.
- Every bullet in the same "形容词 + 不 XX" shape (边缘平滑不割裂, 黑边零伪影).
- Dark tech template with yellow highlight labels on every page.
- Text cards that just split the body into slides.
- Duplicated or padded hashtag lists.

## Writing rules

- Write as the user, in first person, from material they gave. If the motivation or
  experience is missing, ask; do not invent it.
- Short sentences, plain words. At most three emoji in the whole note, none as list
  markers or headings.
- One concrete detail beats three adjectives: a command, a memory number, a before
  and after.
- Body length: a few short paragraphs. The images carry the demo; the body carries
  the why.
- 3–8 hashtags that a reader would actually search, at the end, no duplicates.
- Title: what it is or what it does, 20 characters or fewer, no clickbait punctuation
  stacks like ！！！ or ？？.
